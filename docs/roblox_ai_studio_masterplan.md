## 1. THE SOLO FOUNDER REALITY & THE REVENUE ENGINES

As a single developer operating without a partner, your most valuable resource is **time**. You are a **Systems Architect** and **Product Manager**. Your job is to prompt the AI, manage the logistics, and engineer the conversion metrics.

### Revenue Engine A: Premium Payouts & CC (Concurrent Connections)
Roblox pays developers based on the **engagement time** of Roblox Premium members in your game.

**The Strategy:** Design games that maximize session time. Build AFK-friendly loops, auto-clicking mechanics, and "stay in the game to earn rewards" systems. High CC with 10+ hour session times equals massive passive income.

### Revenue Engine B: In-Experience Purchases (The Psychology of Spending)
- **Gamepasses (Permanent):** High-ticket items (e.g., 499 R$ for Auto-Hatch, 899 R$ for 2x Luck).
- **Developer Products (Consumables):** Low-friction, repeatable purchases (e.g., 25 R$ for an instant stat boost, 99 R$ to skip a timer).

## 4. GAME CONCEPT FRAMEWORKS

### The Golden Criteria
1. ✅ High AFK Potential → Maximizes Premium Payouts
2. ✅ UI/Math Heavy, Map Light → Minimizes complex 3D modeling
3. ✅ Social Flex Mechanics → Players show off to others

### Framework 1: The RNG/Aura Forge (The Premium Payout Farmer)
Click "Roll" → Get aura/title → Combine duplicates in forge → AFK in zone to speed up crafting.

### Framework 2: The Exponential Idle Clicker (The Viral Loop)
Click object → Buy upgrades → Numbers go to trillions → Rebirth for permanent multiplier.

### Framework 3: The Auto-Battler / Gacha (The Whale Engine)
Wait in lobby for gems (AFK) → Summon units → Auto-fight waves.
